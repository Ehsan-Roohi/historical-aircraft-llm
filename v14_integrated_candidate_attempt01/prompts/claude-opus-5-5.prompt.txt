## 2. Mass ledger, extremes and inertia

**Conventions**

- Inertia: Ixx=∫(y²+z²)dm, Iyy=∫(x²+z²)dm, Izz=∫(x²+y²)dm.
- Product: Ixz=∫xz dm. The tensor off-diagonal entry is −Ixz.
- Moments L, M, N follow the right-hand rule about +x (aft), +y (starboard) and +z (up).
  - Positive q is nose-up.
  - Positive p (about the aft axis) raises the starboard wing, i.e. a left roll.
  - Positive r turns the nose to port.
- Mapping to standard stability axes (x forward, y starboard, z down) uses T=diag(−1, 1, −1): p_s=−p, q_s=q, r_s=−r. Ixz is unchanged by this mapping.
