## Scientific scope

The pre-1899 cutoff constrains supplied documents, not model training knowledge. The final Fable candidate has conditional lifting-surface static margins of about 4–10% across eight loading states and approximately 11% heavy-case power reserve under assumed inputs. No design has demonstrated installed six-component trim, validated dynamic modes, structural adequacy or flight capability. These results are not a flightworthiness ranking.
