Scientific correction to restored draft20, 29 September 2026

The complete 61-page master remains preserved. This correction concerns the
V15 Fable lateral surface-model subsection and its dependent numerical results.

The split upper-wing input assigned COMPONENT 2 to the center and COMPONENT 3
to the tip panels. The local AVL manual (analysis/tools/avl/avl_doc.txt,
lines 404-419) explains that surfaces making up one component should share an
identifier; otherwise finite-core treatment alters the mutual influence.

A paired evaluator run held all coordinates, mesh counts, camber, alpha
5.850731893 degrees and the archived tail command fixed. Only the tip's
COMPONENT identifier changed from 3 to 2:

                           CL         Cm          CDind
Original distinct IDs      0.74700    +0.00762     0.0541419
Shared upper-wing ID       0.79678    printed 0    0.0546907
Earlier continuous wing    0.79677    +0.00002     (separate archived run)

This identifies component grouping as the source of the earlier apparent
split-tip lift loss at the tested neutral condition. It does not measure a
real hinge gap, validate the inherited section, or establish physical trim.

Consequences for the paper:
1. Withdraw the interpretation that the 0.79677-to-0.74700 shift itself
   demonstrates a physical-feature representation sensitivity requiring a
   different design trim. It was an evaluator input-setting artifact.
2. Retain the previous expanded-model alpha 6.66238 / tail -3.5075 results
   only as superseded outputs of that input. Do not use their lateral slopes
   as the current corrected-model coefficients until recalculated.
3. Rebuild and check fin/rudder component grouping and overlapping spanwise
   station alignment as well (AVL manual lines 1088-1118). The present paired
   probe changes only upper-wing grouping and does not validate that interface.
4. Recompute trim and static/control/rate derivatives with corrected inputs,
   inspect mesh and perturbation-step sensitivity, and then update the full
   preserved master. No aircraft modes or flight claim follows from this probe.

Raw paired inputs, commands, outputs, SHA-256 hashes and summary are retained
in analysis/results/v15_fable_component_grouping_probe01/. Earlier archives
must remain unchanged for provenance. The complete article must not be
shortened or split again when integrating this correction.
