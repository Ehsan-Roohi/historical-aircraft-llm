# Journal of Aircraft figure-display gate 01

The main article's four-panel V1 lifting-surface comparison was rebuilt from its original SVG as a **typography-only derivative**, enlarged to 6000 by 3950 pixels and placed in landscape. The generic force/moment balance was redrawn as an evaluator SVG and a 4800-by-2240 PNG. It distinguishes applied loads from bookkeeping residuals and labels its force directions *forward/up*, avoiding a false equivalence with each model's aft-positive geometry coordinates. Both source SVGs, their hashes and raster assets are retained in the package. Neither drawing is a measured airplane or a revised V12/V13 three-view.

`analysis/audit_joa_figure_display.py` screens pixel density at the intended *maximum* placed width; its results are in `analysis/results/joa_figure_display_gate01.json`. The revised comparison is at least 694 pixels/inch and the balance schematic at least 738 pixels/inch. The three enhanced V2 three-views are each at least 625 pixels/inch. These pass a numerical 600-ppi line-art screen under the stated placement assumption; this does **not** certify text size, line contrast or final PDF quality. The comparison's 32-pixel source labels would be approximately 8.3 points at 8.64 inches, but only a compiled-page inspection can confirm the actual print size.

The V2 force/moment plate was redrawn as a 1200-unit-wide SVG and 6000-by-6700-pixel display PNG. At its intended 6.5-inch width it has at least 923 pixels/inch, while 21-unit body lettering is approximately 8.2 points. It preserves the frozen V2 values and clearly separates independently retrimmed Astra/Opus states from Fable's unverified model claims; the arrows show directions, not measured centres of pressure. The plate is **not** a depiction of the later V12/V13 installed designs. All six main line-art figures now pass the conservative numerical 600-ppi screen in [AIAA's figure guidance](https://aiaa.org/publications/journals/journal-author/guidelines-for-journal-figures-and-tables/).

The two supplementary response plots were re-rasterized from their unchanged source SVGs at 4800 pixels wide, and the three archival V0 oblique plates from their unchanged SVGs at 6000 pixels wide. The supplementary A4/22-mm-margin layout permits a wider landscape image than the main US-letter article, so the Wright three-view was additionally rendered at 6000 pixels. All nine supplementary line-art PNGs, including three new V0 detail views, now pass the **pixel-density** screen at their respective maximum placed widths. This is not a claim that the full V0 plates are legible in print: a separate source-lettering audit finds that the smallest explicit Astra, Fable and Opus V0 labels would be at most **6.31, 6.88 and 6.02 points**, respectively, even if the images filled the entire permitted width. The height cap could reduce them further. Enlargement of a raster alone does not change physical type size. Each archival plate remains unaltered and has an adjacent 11-point-readable prose component key. The three new researcher-created display derivatives crop the unchanged source geometry, hide only dense source prose and overlay eight numbered markers keyed in adjacent text; they do **not** replace original model outputs or repair the mechanisms. Their marker upper bounds at the permitted width are 13.77, 12.39 and 12.39 points, but the actual compiled placement remains unverified. The original SVGs remain available for zoomed digital inspection. The final compiled PDF must still be inspected page by page for type, contrast, clipping and captions.
