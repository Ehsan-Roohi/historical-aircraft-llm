## 5. Four-root longitudinal and lateral calculation path

Linearize the complete right-handed equations about each actual trim:

\[
m(\dot{\mathbf v}+\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf v)
=\mathbf F+\mathbf F_g,
\]

\[
\mathbf I\dot{\boldsymbol\omega}
+\boldsymbol\omega\times(\mathbf I\boldsymbol\omega+\mathbf H_{\rm rotor})
=\mathbf M-\dot{\mathbf H}_{\rm rotor}.
\]

Use exact force-axis transformations and the signed rotor angular momentum. Include force and moment dependence on acceleration in the descriptor matrix rather than dropping it.

### Longitudinal system

Use

\[
x_{\rm lon}=[\Delta u,\Delta w,\Delta q,\Delta\theta]^T,
\qquad E_{\rm lon}\dot x=A_{\rm lon}x+B_{\rm lon}\Delta c.
\]

At small perturbations, \(\Delta\alpha\) is obtained from the local derivative of \(\tan^{-1}(w/u)\). Consequently the measured \(\dot\alpha\) terms contribute to \(E_{\rm lon}\), including coupling through \(\dot u\) when trim \(w\ne0\).

Solve

\[
\det(A_{\rm lon}-\lambda E_{\rm lon})=0
\]

for four roots. Identify short-period and phugoid modes by eigenvectors, participation and continuation between states—not merely frequency ordering.

### Lateral system

Use

\[

### Important limitation

A tractor installation can break longitudinal/lateral decoupling through torque, asymmetric trim and gyroscopic coupling. Therefore:

- report the requested four-plus-four **block-model** roots;
- also solve the coupled rigid-body descriptor system;
- compare block and coupled results;
- if coupling materially changes the verdict, the eight block roots are only diagnostics.

Adding engine, cable, fuel-slosh, wake or elastic states creates additional roots. Do not force those systems into four roots by deleting their dynamics.

For a complex root \(\lambda=\sigma\pm i\omega_d\), report

\[
\omega_n=\sqrt{\sigma^2+\omega_d^2},\quad
\zeta=-\sigma/\omega_n,\quad
T=2\pi/|\omega_d|.
\]

For stable roots, amplitude e-folding time is \(1/|\sigma|\); for unstable roots, doubling time is \(\ln2/\sigma\). For real roots report the same decay/growth time without an artificial oscillatory damping ratio.
