## 2. Mass ledger, extremes and inertia

**Conventions**

- Inertia: Ixx=∫(y²+z²)dm, Iyy=∫(x²+z²)dm, Izz=∫(x²+y²)dm.
- Product: Ixz=∫xz dm. The tensor off-diagonal entry is −Ixz.
- Moments L, M, N follow the right-hand rule about +x (aft), +y (starboard) and +z (up).
  - Positive q is nose-up.
  - Positive p (about the aft axis) raises the starboard wing, i.e. a left roll.
  - Positive r turns the nose to port.
- Mapping to standard stability axes (x forward, y starboard, z down) uses T=diag(−1, 1, −1): p_s=−p, q_s=q, r_s=−r. Ixz is unchanged by this mapping.

The full ledger is in the JSON. All masses are ASSUMPTIONS, and none has been weighed. Intrinsic inertias are DESIGN-EST for uniform idealised shapes. The seated human modelled as a uniform box is a crude approximation, and its true inertia is UNKNOWN.

**Derivatives required**

- **Longitudinal:** CX_u, CX_α, CZ_u, CZ_α, CZ_q, CZ_α̇, Cm_u, Cm_α, Cm_q, Cm_α̇.
- **Lateral:** CY_β, CY_p, CY_r, Cl_β, Cl_p, Cl_r, Cn_β, Cn_p, Cn_r.
- **Control:** CZ, Cm and CX with respect to δe; Cl, Cn and CY with respect to δf and δr.
- **Propulsion:** T_u, T_δthrottle, the thrust moment arm, propeller normal force versus α and β (P-factor), slipstream increments on the tail, and the gyroscopic h.

**Methods**

1. **Steady derivatives:** AVL or VLM on the V3 geometry with the biplane cellule, nacelle and boom bodies (slender body), plus fin and ventral fin. The evaluator runs this.
2. **Unsteady CL_α̇ and Cm_α̇:** these must NOT be taken from steady AVL.
   - First estimate: the tail downwash-lag formula, Cm_α̇ ≈ −2·a_t·η_t·V_H·(l_t/c)·dε/dα. The inputs dε/dα and a_t come from VLM. The result is an ESTIMATE only.
   - Resolution: a captive-model forced-oscillation test (pitch and plunge separation), or an unsteady panel or frequency-domain model.
3. **Control and slipstream effects:** tow or wind-tunnel tests of a model with the propeller running.
4. **Uncertainty:** each derivative gets a ± band. Absent values stay UNKNOWN and are never set to zero.

## 6. Longitudinal and lateral roots

**Calculation path**

- Longitudinal state [u, w, q, θ]; lateral state [v, p, r, φ].
- Dimensional derivatives use the ledger mass and the Section 2 inertias, with Ixz retained.
- Gyroscopic coupling from h adds a moment of (0, −r·h, +q·h) in the body frame. It couples the two 4×4 blocks, so an 8×8 coupled solution must also be run.
- Run a Monte Carlo sweep over the derivative and inertia bands and report root clouds.

**Mode labels**

- Longitudinal: phugoid (2 roots) and short period (2 roots).
- Lateral: roll subsidence, spiral, and Dutch roll (2 roots).
