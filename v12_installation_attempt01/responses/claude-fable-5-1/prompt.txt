## 3. Inertia and sign convention

Body frame B: x aft, y right, z up, origin at front cross-member centre. Moments: L, M, N positive right-hand about +x, +y, +z, so **positive M is nose-up when x is aft and z is up? No —** with x aft and z up, a right-hand rotation about +y (from z toward x) drops the nose; therefore in this frame positive M is **nose-down**. To keep the V2 statement "pitching moment positive nose-up", V3 reports aerodynamic pitching moment as M_aero = −M_B and states this explicitly; the state-matrix work in §6 must be done in a standard flight frame (x forward, z down) obtained by the rotation R = diag(−1, 1, −1), under which Ixz changes sign. Inertia convention: I_xx = Σm(y²+z²) etc.; products reported as P_xz = Σm·x·z (positive-definite convention), so the tensor off-diagonal is −P_xz. Intrinsic inertias come from named shape models (plate, rod, box, rotation-averaged 2-blade rod); they are not measured and are not inferred from centroids alone. Uncertainty ±30% on each diagonal, ±100% on P_xz, until the bifilar/compound-pendulum test T-M2 is run.
